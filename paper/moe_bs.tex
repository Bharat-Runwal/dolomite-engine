\vspace{-2pt}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{0.50\textwidth}
\begin{wintile}
\textbf{Why fh1 matters.}
At iso-everything, swapping the 3-matrix \texttt{swiglu} expert for the
2-matrix energy gradient $\partial E_e/\partial x$ wins on every headline
metric. The gain is inductive bias, not capacity or routing.
\end{wintile}

\column{0.50\textwidth}
\begin{accenttile}
\textbf{Boltzmann routing still trails fh1.}
All Boltzmann-router variants lose to fh1 on WT-PPL and GSM8K.
\textbf{Fixed $\sqrt{1/d}$ scaling helps most}: Avg $48.9{\to}50.8$,
WT-PPL $29.9{\to}28.1$ vs.\ learnable $T$ --- and edges past the std-MoE
baseline on Avg, though fh1 (51.2) is still ahead.
\end{accenttile}

\end{columns}

\vspace{2pt}
\begin{egpttile}
\textbf{Takeaway.}
Energy-based MoE wins because of energy-based \emph{experts}, not energy-based routing.
\textbf{Next:} confirm at 1\,B-active scale (run in flight).
\end{egpttile}

\vspace{1pt}
{\sffamily\tiny\color{cTextSec}\hfill

$=$ 31.5\,B tokens. \;
Avg $=$ mean of 11 acc-style metrics. \;
\hfill\hfill}
